Teams#

The people behind Scikit-plots#

See also

Scikit-plots is a community project, developed by a large group of people, all across the world. A few core contributor teams, listed below, have central roles, however a more complete list of contributors can be found on github.

Maintainers Team#

The following people are currently maintainers, in charge of consolidating scikit-plots’s development and maintenance:


muhammed çelik


Jurriaan Nagelkerke


David Pilger

Note

Please do not email the authors directly to ask for assistance or report issues. Instead, please see What’s the best way to ask questions about scikit-plots in the FAQ.
